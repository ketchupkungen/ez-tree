# Creature editor — anatomical bases and animated GLB export

Open the local editor, select **Creature**, then choose Wolf, Elk, Snake, Lizard or Fish in **Presets**. The original EZ-Tree panel is retained. The creature preview defaults to a clean lit stage; **Studio View** returns it to the original scene when turned off.

## Shape controls

Each species exposes relevant proportions: body length/build, limb length, head/neck/muzzle/ear size, tail length, elk antlers or fish fins. Colors and seeded variation change the coat/skin. **Mesh detail** is 0 (draft), 1 (balanced/default), or 2 (fine). **Fit View** frames the animal in the area beside the panel. Snake framing uses a higher angle so its body curvature is visible.

Torso, neck, limbs and tails are blended into a continuous implicit surface. Eyes, hooves, antlers and fins are separately constructed details merged into the same skinned mesh. Colors are per vertex; there are no external animal textures, imported assets or fur simulations. These are procedural anatomical starting points with stylized materials.

## Rig and animation

| Preset | Rig bones | Exported clips |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Wolf | 23 | Idle, Walk |
| Elk | 23 | Idle, Walk |
| Snake | 20 | Idle, Slither |
| Lizard | 23 | Idle, Walk |
| Fish | 8 | Idle, Swim |

Open **Rig & Animation** to choose locomotion, Idle or Rest pose; toggle **Playing** to pause/resume. **Cycle speed** changes the baked cycle duration. **Stride length** changes the quadruped step; **Body sway** changes tail/body movement. **Show Rig** overlays the bone hierarchy. Shape edits regenerate the rig and skin weights for the revised anatomy.

Quadruped walking clips contain sampled two-link leg IK and planted/swing foot paths. Snake/fish clips drive a chain of axial bones with traveling waves. All cycles play in place, with gentle posture motion; they do not move the character through the scene. Ground adaptation, contacts on uneven terrain and physics are not implemented.

## Save and export

- **Save Preset / Load Preset:** version 2 creature recipes store the species, shape, colors, seed and gait parameters. Older placeholder recipes migrate to Wolf/Lizard/Elk; their old arbitrary body proportions are not reproduced.
- **Export GLB (Full Detail):** exports the skinned animal, named skeleton, vertex colors, Idle and species locomotion clips. This includes both clips even if the preview is paused or showing Rest pose. It excludes the stage, forest and debug skeleton overlay.
- **Export PNG:** transparent image of the current pose, without the stage or bone overlay.

Exports are built from a fresh bind pose. Preview scale/offset are baked into vertices, joint positions and root-position keys so the exported skin does not depend on parent transforms. Clip speed/stride/sway edits are baked; the preview Playing/Rest pose selections are not exported as additional clips.

## Verified and remaining scope

The production app builds. Eleven Node tests and four browser scenarios passed. The rig tests check normalized, finite weights; valid bone references; loop endpoints; finite deformations; all species at parameter extremes; recipe migration; and clip-speed baking. Existing tree/plant/forest workflows remain covered.

All five exported examples passed the official Khronos glTF Validator with **0 errors and 0 warnings**. A separate GLTFLoader reload verified the bind pose and sampled moving vertices from the locomotion clips. The five anatomies, example rig overlays and camera framing were visually inspected. Blender, Unreal, Unity and Godot imports have not been verified in this pass.

The editor currently uses five anatomical plans and proportion controls. Spore-style arbitrary body-part placement, direct sculpting, manual skin-weight painting and full animation authoring remain future work. The surface topology is generated by marching tetrahedra rather than artist-authored deformation loops; extreme proportions or poses can still need cleanup before production use.

Implementation: `creature/creatures.js` defines anatomy, `creature/surface.js` meshes and weights it, and `creature/locomotion.js` generates bone clips. `modes.js` owns the live mixer, stage and export bind-pose conversion. Three.js SkinnedMesh, AnimationMixer and GLTFExporter provide the runtime/export foundation.
